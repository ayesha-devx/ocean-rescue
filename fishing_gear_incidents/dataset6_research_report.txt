URL   : https://www.ghostgear.org/

                Regional coverage appears sparse for the study bounding box.
Download path : Manual map-based browsing, or account registration for bulk export.
Licence       : Not explicitly stated for raw data; citation required.
Access request: Create account at ghostgear.org or email
                info@worldanimalprotection.org for bulk data queries.
RESULT        : NO DIRECT DOWNLOAD for study region without account/formal request.

SOURCE 3 - Olive Ridley Project (ORP)
URL   : https://oliveridleyproject.org/report-a-ghost-net/
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access status : Website fully live. UK-registered charity.
What they hold: One of the most comprehensive ghost-net databases in the
                Indian Ocean, collected via dive surveys (Maldives, Oman),
                partner reports (fishers, coast guards), and a public form.
Arabian Sea   : ORP has documented incidents offshore Pakistan (WWF-Pakistan
                partnership) and off the Malabar Coast. Published papers
                (Stelfox et al. 2016; Duncan et al. 2017) reference incidents
                in the northern Indian Ocean adjacent to the study region.
Public data   : NO bulk CSV or shapefile available to the public.
                Data is proprietary and published in aggregated/anonymised form
                in peer-reviewed papers only.
How to access : Email info@oliveridleyproject.org with a research proposal.
                Academic data-sharing MOU expected; turnaround 4-8 weeks.
Published proxy: Stelfox et al. (2016) Marine Pollution Bulletin 109(1):606-615.
                 Contains Maldivian/IOTC-area coordinates but does NOT cover
                 the exact 68-74E corridor.
RESULT        : NO DIRECT DOWNLOAD. Real records exist but require formal MOU.

SOURCE 4 - FAO Global ALDFG Survey
URL   : https://www.fao.org/responsible-fishing/marking-of-fishing-gear/
        fao-global-aldfg-survey/en
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access status : FAO portal accessible.
What they hold: Standardised questionnaire and online data-entry portal for
                national/regional ALDFG assessments. Data co-owned by FAO
                and the survey partner (country/institution).
Arabian Sea   : Indian partner is ICAR-CIFT (Kochi). Survey efforts underway
                as of 2024-2025. Pakistan survey status unclear.
                FAO-IMO GloLitter Partnerships Project (2021-2026) assisting
                India and Pakistan, but outputs not yet publicly released.
Public data   : The FAO portal is an INPUT system, not a public DOWNLOAD system.
How to access : Email fishing-technology@fao.org. Or contact ICAR-CIFT Kochi.
RESULT        : NO DIRECT DOWNLOAD. Zero rows available immediately.

SOURCE 5 - Marine Debris Tracker (NOAA / Univ. Georgia)
URL   : https://www.debristracker.org/
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access status : Website live; iOS/Android app operational.
What they hold: Global citizen-science marine debris database (all categories).
                Over 15 million records globally.
Arabian Sea   : Database is NOT ALDFG-specific. Items include "fishing line",
                "fishing net", "buoy", "rope" - may overlap with ALDFG but
                cannot be distinguished from active gear without case review.
Download      : Account creation required. Bulk download via
                debristracker101@gmail.com. Open-licensed (CC BY) once obtained.
Relevance     : PARTIAL. Records represent general marine debris, not confirmed
                ALDFG. Study region has sparse user coverage.
RESULT        : NO IMMEDIATE DOWNLOAD. Requires account + email request.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOURCE 6 - Ocean Conservancy TIDES / International Coastal Cleanup (ICC)
URL   : https://www.coastalcleanupdata.org/
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access status : Website live. ICC data available since 1986.
What they hold: World's largest marine-litter database. Annual cleanup counts
                by debris category. India and Pakistan both participate in ICC.
Arabian Sea   : Data covers coastal states (Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Kerala,
                Karnataka; Sindh, Balochistan). However:
                (a) Locations are beach transects, NOT offshore coordinates.
                (b) Gear categories cover nets, line, buoys, traps collected
                    from beaches - NOT georeferenced offshore ALDFG incidents.
                (c) Year-level national aggregates, not point data.
Download      : Country/year reports downloadable without registration (PDF/Excel).
                Point-level events require free account.
Relevance     : LOW for ALDFG drift modelling. No offshore lat/lon coordinates
                suitable for drift-model initialisation.
RESULT        : NOT SUITABLE as ALDFG point data. Secondary/contextual use only.
