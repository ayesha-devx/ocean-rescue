## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of rigorous autonomous ocean intelligence and high-impact environmental stewardship. Built for research institutions, maritime authorities, venture philanthropists, and offshore recovery crews, the aesthetic bridges sophisticated marine editorial publishing with precise modern climate-tech telemetry.

The experience evokes:
- **Scientific Authority:** Uncompromising clarity and precision reminiscent of marine cartography and academic monographs.
- **Urgent Optimism:** Dynamic high-clarity accents against deep aquatic tones that convey active ecological intervention rather than passive observation.
- **Architectural Poise:** Asymmetrical compositions, delicate rectilinear framing, and generous negative space that provide quiet confidence for high-density geospatial and environmental data.

The design movement synthesizes **Modern Climate-Tech Editorial** with subtle nautical technicality: expansive pure-white expanses, alternating ice-hued datum planes, crisp structural keylines, and sharp typography anchored by deep abyssal navy tones.

## Colors

The palette derives strictly from varied ocean strata—ranging from sunlit surface foam to dark benthic depths:

- **Aqua (`#00C9C1`)**: Primary interactive focal point. Applied to critical conversion triggers, real-time sonar detection pings, autonomous recovery vehicle paths, and active tracking status states.
- **Ocean Teal (`#087E8B`)**: Secondary interactive and supporting anchor. Used for secondary actions, data filters, selected telemetry cards, and navigational accents.
- **Navy Text (`#173B4D`)**: Foundational structural ink. Governs primary and secondary typography, stroke dividers, and vector iconography to eliminate harsh pure black while maintaining pristine WCAG AAA contrast against light grounds.
- **Ice White (`#F2FAFA`)**: Alternating architectural canvas. Establishes clear section shifts, elevation containers, and secondary surface layers without visual weight.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Dominant viewport background ensuring clinical precision and breathing room.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas / Ground**: `#FFFFFF` for primary base; `#F2FAFA` for recessed modules, secondary surfaces, and alternating bands.
- **Border & Outlines**: Low-opacity Navy (`rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.12)`) for subtle bounding containers; solid `#00C9C1` or `#087E8B` strictly reserved for active tracking status or focus states.
- **Text & Hierarchy**: Primary headings and critical telemetry run in `#173B4D`; contextual and secondary labels use `#173B4D` at 70% opacity; disabled elements at 35% opacity.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial poise of **Playfair Display** with the clean, highly legible functional clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Gives ocean conservation initiatives academic gravitas and timeless literary presence. Headings never use faux bolding; they rely on calibrated weights (500/600) with tightened tracking to ensure elegance.
- **Telemetry & Body Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides crisp geometric performance on tactical mapping interfaces, sensor feeds, and mission logs.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Set strictly in uppercase with extended letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to resemble scientific apparatus indices, vessel callsigns, and geographic coordinate designations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system (desktop) that shifts gracefully to 8 columns (tablet) and 4 columns (mobile), enforcing generous negative space to evoke oceanic expansiveness.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Stacks modular cards vertically; editorial displays swap to designated `-mobile` scales.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`. 
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, max page bound of `1440px`, with dynamic lateral gutters and an outer minimum margin of `3rem`.

### Asymmetric Image Framing & Organic Dividers
- Imagery must reject standard symmetrical card grids. Visual assets rely on offset rectangular border frames: an image container will sit offset inside an exterior `#00C9C1` hairline accent box with a uniform 12px or 16px lateral reveal.
- Transition between full-width `#FFFFFF` zones and `#F2FAFA` zones utilizes subtle continuous SVG wave paths (amplitude under 24px) or straight hairline datum cuts accented by a single miniature coordinate notch.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from heavy drop shadows, utilizing **tonal stratums, translucent coastal blurs, and low-contrast keylines** to create elevation reminiscent of nautical instruments and clear waters:

- **Level 0 (Abyssal Base)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` canvas. Flat, unbordered.
- **Level 1 (Shelf / Ice Layer)**: `#F2FAFA` surface planes with a single razor-thin border (`1px solid rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.08)`). No drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active Telemetry Card)**: `#FFFFFF` floating over `#F2FAFA`. Elevation is achieved via an ambient, tinted shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.04), 0 2px 6px rgba(8, 126, 139, 0.03)` complemented by a crisp `1px` perimeter border (`rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.1)`).
- **Level 3 (Tactical Float / Navigation)**: Frosted glass finish across maritime controls and headers: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.08)`.
- **Target Accent Elevation**: Active ALDFG targets utilize an outer glow rather than a standard blur: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #00C9C1, 0 4px 20px rgba(0, 201, 193, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`0.25rem` / 4px base border radius). This maintains technical discipline and nautical instrument precision, explicitly rejecting overly bubbly or hyper-rounded interfaces.

- **Standard Containers & Data Cards**: `4px` (`0.25rem`). Maintains crisp architectural definition.
- **Panels & Dialogs (`rounded-lg`)**: `8px` (`0.5rem`). Applied to major map overlay panels and recovery operation dossiers.
- **Badges & Micro-tags**: `2px` or sharp `0px` with uppercase tracked text, reinforcing scientific taxonomy tags.
- **Interactive Targets**: Primary CTA buttons employ a minimal `4px` radius or razor-sharp architectural corners with an offset accent line.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Aqua)**:
  - Base: `#00C9C1` solid fill, `#173B4D` bold label, `4px` radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#02B8B1` with a slight upward translation (`-1px`) and a crisp `0 4px 14px rgba(0, 201, 193, 0.35)` glow.
- **Secondary Action (Teal)**:
  - Base: `#087E8B` solid fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#066772`.
- **Ghost / Outlined Accent**:
  - Base: Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.2)`, `#173B4D` text.
  - Hover: Border switches to `#087E8B`, font shifts to `#087E8B`, background fills with `rgba(8, 126, 139, 0.04)`.

### Chips & Tactical Status Badges
- **Status Chips (e.g., "Sonar Verified", "Retrieval In-Progress")**:
  - Background: `#F2FAFA`; border: `1px solid rgba(8, 126, 139, 0.2)`.
  - Label: `0.6875rem` (`label-sm`), `#173B4D`, uppercase, tracked `0.06em`.
  - Indicator: Preceded by a `6px` solid indicator dot (`#00C9C1` for active detection, `#087E8B` for autonomous mission underway).

### Mission Dossier & Metric Cards
- Base: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.08)`. Radius: `4px`.
- Padding: `1.5rem`.
- Feature Variant: Top border features an integrated `2px` accent line in `#00C9C1`. When selected, border switches fully to `1px solid #087E8B` with a subtle interior Ice White wash (`#F2FAFA`).

### Text Inputs & Telemetry Filters
- Base: `#FFFFFF` interior, `1px solid rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.16)` border, `4px` radius, padding: `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography: `body-md` in `#173B4D`.
- Focus: Border immediately sharpens to `#087E8B` with an offset outline ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 201, 193, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Box: `18px x 18px`, `2px` radius. Default border is `1.5px solid rgba(23, 59, 77, 0.3)`.
- Selected: `#087E8B` background, pure white vector checkmark. Focus ring matches `#00C9C1`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **ALDFG Recovery Coordinates Widget**: Rectilinear grid panel featuring miniature mono-spaced latitude/longitude readouts, depth gauges in fathoms/meters, and an asymmetric rectangular preview frame displaying AI-enhanced side-scan sonar imagery.
- **Wave Transition Divider**: Custom horizontal SVG boundary component that seamlessly transitions page sections using oceanographic bathymetry curves in `#F2FAFA` against `#FFFFFF`.
