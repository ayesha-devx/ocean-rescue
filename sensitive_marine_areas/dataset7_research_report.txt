--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & RESEARCH OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the OceanRescue project, drift forecasting identifies where ALDFG (ghost nets)
accumulate, but retrieval planning requires evaluating ecological risk. Ghost gear
retrieval operations in sensitive marine habitats (coral reefs, seagrass beds, bird
sanctuaries, turtle nesting beaches) carry dual risks:
  (a) Environmental impact of unrecovered ALDFG (ghost fishing, reef smothering).
  (b) Secondary disturbance during recovery operations (anchor damage, net snagging).

To enable automated risk weighting in retrieval path planning algorithms, Dataset 7
provides verified geospatial vector layers (polygons, centroids, and boundaries)
of marine and coastal protected areas.

Key Findings of the Audit:
  - Primary Marine Sanctuary in Study Bbox: Malvan Marine Sanctuary (Sindhudurg,
    Maharashtra; 16.04°–16.08°N, 73.44°–73.49°E; area ~29.12 km², established 1987,
    IUCN Category IV).
  - OpenStreetMap (OSM) Coverage: 37 named protected area features harvested within
    and immediately adjacent to the study region bounding box via Overpass API.
  - Jurisdictional Boundary: India Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) polygon segment
    spanning 14°–20°N, 68°–74°E (MRGID 8480, Marine Regions reference).
  - Broader Arabian Sea Context: 12 key marine & coastal protected areas compiled
    across the wider Northern Indian Ocean / Arabian Sea region for model context.

5. INTEGRATION RECOMMENDATIONS FOR OCEANRESCUE PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Environmental Sensitivity Weighting:
   - When ALDFG drift trajectory predictions cross into Malvan Marine Sanctuary
     (16.04°–16.08°N, 73.44°–73.49°E), trigger high-priority recovery alert (Weight 1.0).
   - Near estuarine bird sanctuaries (Thane Creek, Salim Ali), set buffer risk alerts (Weight 0.7).

2. Recovery Planning Constraints:
   - Non-destructive retrieval protocols (manual dive retrieval vs mechanized trawling)
     must be flagged whenever retrieval coordinates fall inside IUCN Cat IV MPAs.
