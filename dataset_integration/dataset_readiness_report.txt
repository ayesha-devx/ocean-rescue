4. SCIENTIFIC, TECHNICAL & LICENSING LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Physical Limitations : Windage coefficient ($C_w$) for floating ALDFG varies by buoyancy/submersion
                           (0.01 to 0.04). Stokes drift from wave period ($VTM10$) must be combined with $u10, v10$.
  - Incident Limitation  : No real ALDFG incident dataset exists for the 14–20°N, 68–74°E study region.
                           The 50 synthetic points are labeled for testing drift algorithms only.
  - Protected Areas Limit: Malvan Marine Sanctuary is an approximate rectangle. Actual reef boundaries
                           require high-res shapefiles from UNEP-WCMC.
  - Licensing Summary    :
    * Copernicus Marine (Currents & Waves) : Free open access (CMEMS License).
    * ECMWF ERA5 (New Wind) : Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) Open License.
    * GEBCO 2026 (Bathymetry) : GEBCO Sub-Committee on Undersea Feature Names (Open License).
    * OpenStreetMap (OSM) : ODbL (Open Database License) (c) OpenStreetMap contributors.
    * TrashCan Dataset : Open academic license for underwater computer vision research.
